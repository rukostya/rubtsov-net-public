CPO Team and product strategy

Product Leadership

I am interested in building both individual products and an environment where strong products emerge consistently.

At T-Bank, I led the development of SCALE — a framework for assessing product managers’ professional level.

The framework helps define expectations at different professional levels, assess the scale of results, task complexity, autonomy, and leadership, and provide specific feedback and plan professional development.

I also worked on hiring processes, helped create a product strategy course, and mentored colleagues.
